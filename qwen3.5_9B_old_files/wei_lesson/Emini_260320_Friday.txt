This is RTH 5 minute chart of E-mini provided by Al Brooks.

To conclude the context of the whole day, it can be called "Bear Trend Day" (Al Brooks didn't use this words in this chart). Al Brooks mentioned it as "Gap Down, then sideways and then bear trend resumption".

(Note that yesterday is Thursday, Mar 19 2026, [image:Emini_260319_Thursday].)

The day started by gap down below bar#74 of yesterday which is a bear breakout and could be measured to the low of bar#2 by 29.00 points. With this breakout range, it was possible at the moment that bar#68 of yesterday was a bull trap, especially when bar#68-69-70 of yesterday was an ii which is a good context for reversal (you can search for the meaning of ii in Al Brooks' books). Bar#1 was a good short signal bar and bar#2 was also a follow-through selling, so the context of the bear trend day was possible. Bar#4 was a low 1 short setup and double top below 20EMA after good context of the bears. The market broke below bar#2's low to bar#9's low and also broke below Yesterday's low and Overnight low (See in chart). The word "Overnight" refers to globex session. At bar#9, the probability of the bears controlling the market increased to 60%. Anyway, after being a sell climax and the bulls tried to make a round bottom and wedge bottom {bar#7-9-17}.

(Note that when I use braces {bar#a-b-c}, "a" refers to the high/low of the first push, "b" refers to the high/low of the second push, and "c" refers to the high/low of the third push of the wedge both wedge top and wedge bottom.)

Firstly, bar#18 to bar#21 was a two-leg pullback to test 20EMA and the bear shorted above bar#19's high at 20EMA hoping for still-intact bear channel starting since the high of bar#8 and expecting a failed bull breakout above bar#19's high and expecting a bear flag or wedge top [bar#16-19-21] at 20EMA. Bar#22 confirmed their expectation, but suddenly, the bulls tried to make upside breakout above bar#21's high by bar#23. They tried to make this bear flag to be failed by 9.25 points upside breakout above bar#21's high. Then there was the deep pullback (Failed bull breakout) and then channel up. Note again that this pattern is called "Spike, Pullback and Channel" by Al Brooks which bar#23 is a bull "Spike". The up-tail of bar#23 and bar#24 was a deep "Pullback" in smaller timeframe. Bar#25 to bar#35 was a "Channel" up. With these three parts, "Spike, Pullback and Channel" is a common pattern in any chart. It often confuses traders because of deep pullback but at last it channel up to test the prior high/low of the spike.

After testing bar#23's high, bar#35 was a low short setup above 20EMA and double top lower high with bar#23's high failing at 20EMA. Anyway, when you see the second and third green arrow in this chart, we could assume that Al Brooks also saw the pattern of "Spike, Pullback and Channel" and hoping going up above bar#23's high. So he bought bull breakout at bar#30 and again at bar#35. We cannot see any red arrow in this area. That means Al Brooks thought that the market was still going up. He started to short bar#37 after two consecutive bear bars, bar#35 and bar#36. There was the bear trend resumption in the form of small pullback bear trend since bar#35 to the end of day and rebound after reaching measured move target (yellow line). You can also see Al Brooks pointed out at bar#59, a strong bear breakout bar, "Bear trend resumes, Do not buy". That means the bears were dominating the market. The eager bulls who bought at any price would be trapped and would be dragged down to bar#74. Note that around measured move target, the market was a parabolic wedge bottom (purple line) which was a type of sell climax. And the time was near the end of day session, the market should rebound by "End of day profit taking" as Al Brooks pointed out.
