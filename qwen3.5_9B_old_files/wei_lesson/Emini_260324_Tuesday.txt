[Context of the whole day]: Tranding Range Day (most of the bars overlapped each others), by gap down on the open, then bull trend and then trading range for the rest of the day.

The distance of big gap down from bar#79's low of Monday (see IMAGE 2, [image: Connected_Emini_260323-260324]) to bar#1's low of Tuesday is breakout range of 37.00 points following by breakout range below bar#1's low to bar#3's low by 10.50 points. At this stage, it was likely that the bears were controlling the market. Anyway, {pwb#79-1-3} is a sell climax, far below 20EMA, and likely to have pullback to test 20EMA. There was indeed a pullback {wt#2-9-16} and sub-wedge top {wt#11-14-16} as a bear flag. The bulls tried to turn this bear flag to be failed by bar#17. Upside breakout was weak and forming another {wt#17-24-29}. Whole bull trend pullback is in pattern of consecutive wedge top starting from bar#5 to bar#29.

There was a pullback of this bull trend which is bar#29 to bar#52 but this pullback was too strong by considering two-leg move breakout below bar#39's low which is breakout range of 16.75 points. There was a second leg up of prior bull trend but, with the effect of breakout range, two-leg move up to bar#62 was a bull trap, by just testing prior high of day (bar#29's high) and 20EMA of 1-hour chart twice (see the blue dotted line on IMAGE 2), even if breakout above bar#55's high to bar#62's high exceeds breakout range. It was likely a second leg bull trap. So most of the bars of day were overlapped each others. We could confirm it a trading range day (or pullback day of possible 2-Day broad bear channel).
