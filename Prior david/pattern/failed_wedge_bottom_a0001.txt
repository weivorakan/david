[PATTERN_EXPLANATION]:
Image 1, [image:failed_wedge_bottom_a0001], is a 5 minute chart of GOLD or XAUUSD and it is a pattern of failed wedge bottom which is a failed reversal pattern.

Wedge bottom consists of three pushes down or three legs as following;
Bar 14 to bar 17 is the first leg.
Bar 25 to bar 27 is the second leg.
Bar 42 to bar 52 is the third leg.

Note: Average bar size of 5 minute chart of GOLD or XAUUSD on 23 April 2026 is around 5.21 points.

Wedge bottom can also be called as a wedge bull flag or a pullback in a bull trend. But, sometimes, even if it is a good buy signal for resumption of prior bull trend, it could be failed.

Bear breakout which starts from bar 26 to bar 27 is far enough, the distance between the low of bar 17 to the low of bar 27 is 6.75 points which exceeds the breakout range value. So, it is likely that there would probably be more sellers than buyers near the high of bar 25 or forming a bear channel which is a type of trading range.

When the market goes down again to test the low of bar 27, the two-leg-move since bar 49 to bar 52 creates uncertainty of bear breakout because it consists of bad follow-through bars, bar 50 and bar 52. The bears know that with this weak type of bear trend, the market could go up again and being able to go up higher than most bears expect, by the effect of prior bull trend (bar 1 to bar 14). So some bears might take their profit near the low of bar 27.

The bull leg from bar 52 to bar 61 is very strong (By the effect of wedge bottom) and it looks like it is going up to the high of bar 14 by the time of 23:30-23:45, and it shall be called 'Big Up'. Even if it is as strong as seen, the bear move from bar 43 to bar 52 is in a form of strong tight bear channel, and it shall be called 'Big Down'. One important context learned from Al Brooks is 'Big Down, Big Up, Big Confusion', a sign of trading range price action which should be traded as 'Buy-low-Sell-high-and-Scalp' technique.

The bull move from bar 52 to bar 61 just reaches the measured move target measured by the height of a small bear flag (bar 52 to bar 56) and it doesn't go up further much more after reaching this measured move target.

Note: The distance between the low of bar 52 and the high of bar 56 creates measured move target at 4734.85.

The high of bar 61 doesn't quite reach the bear trend line pivoting from the high of bar 14 to the high of bar 42. By the context of trading range or bear channel, there should be a lot of sellers near the high of bar 42.

After bar 63 and bar 64, there is a successful bear breakout to bar 71 which also exceeds the measured move target measured by the height of this failed wedge bottom (from high of bar 14 to low of bar 52 which you can measure by yourself).
