In any complex legs, the second leg and the third leg are often considered as sub-two-leg-move. In any stair pattern, the bears see the weakness of the bulls and they tends to short near the high of the prior leg to form a double top pattern which will form a wedge top pattern too. Bar 25, a decent bear bar closing near its low, forms the pattern of wedge top and higher high double top.

Note that when breakout range is near the average bar size value in that timeframe (more than 50%), the breakout range should be considered.
