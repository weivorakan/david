Bar 13 to bar 19 is the second leg.
Bar 23 to bar 29 is the third leg.

Wedge top can also be called as a wedge bear flag or a pullback in a bear trend. So, sometimes, the first leg of wedge top can be started counting while the last leg of prior bear leg (bar 9 to bar 13) didn't finish yet.

In any complex legs, the second leg and the third leg are often considered as sub-two-leg-move. Because the second leg is quite strong (Bar 13 to bar 19 is in a form of tight bull channel) and breakout above the high of the first leg, which is the high of bar 9, is far enough (From the bar of bar 9 to the high of bar 19 is 8.00 points) so that it looks like a successful upside breakout during the time of 12:50-13:00. However the strength of the bulls in negative gap is a stair pattern (no visible gap) and it is not the stronger type of bull trend. In any stair pattern, the bears see the weakness of the bulls and they tends to short near the high of the prior leg to form a double top pattern which will form a wedge top pattern too. Bar 29, a bear bar, forms the pattern of wedge top and higher high double top.

Normally, the start of resumption of prior bear trend shall happen since bar 27 and the market shall go down to test the low of bar 13. But, sometimes, the market is tricky and beguile that it is going to have upside breakout (bar 43 is the attempt of upside breakout and making this wedge top to be failed) but it fails later as shown by bar 44, a good short signal bar. That's why bar 43 and bar 44 shall be called as 'a failed breakout above wedge top'. Then the resumption of the bear trend starts from bar 44 to bar 56.
