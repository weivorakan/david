Image 1, [image:wedge_top_a0001], is a 5 minute chart of GOLD or XAUUSD and it is a pattern of wedge top which is a reversal pattern.

Wedge top consists of three pushes up or three legs as following;
Bar 6 to bar 10 is the first leg.
Bar 15 to bar 21 is the second leg.
Bar 24 to bar 27 is the third leg.

In any complex legs, the second leg and the third leg are often considered as sub-two-leg-move. Because the second leg is quite strong (Bar 15 to bar 21 is in a form of tight bull channel) and breakout above the high of the first leg, which is the high of bar 10, is far enough (7.10 points in 5 minutes chart of XAUUSD) so that it looks like a successful upside breakout during the time of 07:20-07:25. So, it is likely to have another small leg up which is the third leg (the bulls will normally try again).

The pullback after bar 21 is quite deep. It goes below the high of the first leg and starting to form a stair pattern. The bulls tries to keep the negative gap or the body gap opened. The close of bar 24 is higher than the close of bar 24. In a 'LINE CHART', this shows gap which means the strength of the bulls. However the strength of the bulls in negative gap is a stair pattern (no visible gap) and it is not the stronger type of bull trend. In any stair pattern, the bears see the weakness of the bulls and they tends to short near the high of the prior leg to form a double top pattern which will form a wedge top pattern too. Bar 27, a bear bar, forms the pattern of wedge top and higher high double top.

Normally, the start of resumption of prior bear trend shall happen since bar 27 and the market shall go down to test the low of bar 6. But, sometimes, the market is tricky and beguile that it is going to have upside breakout (bar 29 and bar 30 is the attempt of upside breakout and making this wedge top to be failed) but it fails later as shown by bar 30, a good short signal bar. That's why bar 30 shall be called as 'a failed breakout above wedge top'. Then the resumption of the bear trend starts from bar 30 to bar 35.
